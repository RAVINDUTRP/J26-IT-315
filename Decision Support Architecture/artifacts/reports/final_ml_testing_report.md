## 1. Executive Summary

This report documents the design, implementation, empirical training, evaluation, explainability analysis, and offline inference verification of **Component 04: AI-Based Disaster Decision Support Framework**.

Component 04 consumes upstream water contamination predictions generated by **Component 03** (`contamination_probability`, `prediction_confidence`, `contamination_risk_category`) and contextualizes them within a multi-factor Disaster Risk Reduction (DRR) framework. By fusing contamination hazard information with socio-hydrological vulnerability metrics, critical water infrastructure exposure tiers, wireless LoRa mesh communication telemetry health, and emergency response resource constraints, the framework classifies disaster response urgency into four standardized operational tiers:
- **P1 (Critical)**: Immediate emergency response required (intake gate closure, public alert).
- **P2 (High)**: Expedited action required (water source switching, priority laboratory chemical sampling).
- **P3 (Moderate)**: Standard response (targeted field sampling, advisory notices).
- **P4 (Low)**: Routine monitoring (no active intervention).

The machine learning pipeline has been validated across 4,000 synthetic scenarios, 5-fold cross-validation, 1,000 held-out test scenarios, 17 automated unit tests, 10 offline edge-case inference verification tests, and 7 executable Jupyter notebooks.

## 3. Dataset Architecture & Validation

### 3.1 Feature Schema (14 Core Inputs)
1. `contamination_probability` (float `[0.0, 1.0]`): Upstream contamination risk prediction from Component 03.
2. `contamination_risk_category` (categorical `LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH`): Component 03 categorical risk bin.
3. `prediction_confidence` (float `[0.0, 1.0]`): Epistemic certainty of Component 03 prediction.
4. `estimated_exposed_population` (float `[10, 200000]`): Downstream consumer population relying on water intake.
5. `infrastructure_importance` (categorical `CRITICAL, MAJOR, STANDARD, MINOR`): Criticality tier of exposed intake or treatment asset.
6. `communication_status` (categorical `ONLINE, DEGRADED, OFFLINE`): Health of LoRa wireless telemetry mesh.
7. `communication_delay_seconds` (float `[0.0, 86400.0]`): Transmission latency from boat sensor to ground station.
8. `rainfall_mm` (float `[0.0, 300.0]`): Antecedent 24h precipitation causing overland contaminant wash off.
9. `environmental_change_score` (float `[-1.0, 1.0]`): Multi-sensor ambient drift index.
10. `alternative_water_available` (binary `0 or 1`): Auxiliary municipal reservoir bypass availability.
11. `inspection_team_available` (binary `0 or 1`): On-call rapid chemical response team availability.
12. `treatment_resource_available` (binary `0 or 1`): Mobile chemical neutralization/filtration availability.
13. `sensor_data_freshness_seconds` (float `[0.0, 86400.0]`): Elapsed age of the sensor telemetry observation.
14. `missing_input_count` (integer `[0, 10]`): Detected missing or imputed sensor input fields.

- **Scenario Composition**: Normal baseline (45%), moderate runoff (30%), acute contamination surges (25%), communication blackouts (12%), stale telemetry (15%), and resource-constrained crises (18%).

## 4. Domain Feature Engineering

Five domain-specific interaction metrics are derived prior to model ingestion:

1. **Socio-Hydrological Exposure Index ($REI$)**:
   $$REI = P_{contam} \times \log_{10}(\max(Pop, 10))$$
2. **Critical Infrastructure Hazard Product ($IHI$)**:
   $$IHI = P_{contam} \times W_{infra} \quad (W_{infra} \in \{CRITICAL: 1.0, MAJOR: 0.70, STANDARD: 0.40, MINOR: 0.15\})$$
3. **Telemetry Staleness Index ($TSI$)**:
   $$TSI = 0.5 \times \min\left(\frac{Delay}{3600}, 1.0\right) + 0.5 \times \min\left(\frac{Freshness}{7200}, 1.0\right)$$
4. **Precipitation Runoff Wash Hazard ($PRH$)**:
   $$PRH = P_{contam} \times \left(\frac{Rainfall}{50.0}\right)$$
5. **Operational Resource Bottleneck Flag**:
   $$\text{Flag} = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if } P_{contam} \ge 0.50 \text{ and } (\text{alt\_water} + \text{insp\_team} + \text{treatment}) \le 1 \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$

## 5. Experimental Model Training & Cross-Validation

### 5.1 Preprocessing Pipeline
Implemented via a leak-free scikit-learn `ColumnTransformer` fitted exclusively on training scenarios:
- Numerical features: `SimpleImputer(strategy="median")` + `StandardScaler()`.
- Categorical features: `SimpleImputer(strategy="most_frequent")` + `OneHotEncoder(handle_unknown="ignore")`.
- Binary features: `SimpleImputer(strategy="most_frequent")`.

## 12. Known Limitations & Operational Constraints

1. **Synthetic Data Prioritization Policy**: The training labels are derived from a multi-criteria Disaster Risk Reduction heuristic. While this proves algorithmic feasibility, sensitivity, and calibration, real-world deployment requires fine-tuning on historical civil defense operational data.
2. **Decision-Support Boundary**: The model outputs priority classifications and actionable explanations for human commanders. It must **never** trigger autonomous physical actions (such as closing intake floodgates) without human authorization.
3. **Telemetry Age Awareness**: Telemetry observations older than 30 minutes are flagged as stale. Commanders must corroborate stale-data predictions with field confirmation before allocating high-cost resources.
